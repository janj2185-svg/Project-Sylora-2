# SYLORA Canonical Product Design

**Status:** visual source of truth  
**Reference:** `docs/design/sylora-canonical-ui-reference.png`

All SYLORA product surfaces must evolve from the supplied canonical reference. A feature may introduce a new composition required by its job, but it must not introduce a competing visual language.

## Visual DNA

- Light, warm ivory/pearl canvas; the product must never default to a dark cyberpunk shell.
- Luminous flowing ribbons behave like a living environmental layer, not decorative wallpaper pasted independently on every screen.
- Restrained precious-metal gold is paired with pearlescent violet, cyan, rose and mint spectral light.
- Glass surfaces are soft, translucent and spatially layered with subtle borders/shadows; dense opaque dashboard chrome is avoided.
- Photography/video remains the strongest high-contrast content while surrounding UI stays calm and luminous.
- The only product lockup is `public/assets/brand/canonical/SYLORA_CANONICAL_LOGO_MASTER.png`: black four-point crystalline mark, champagne-gold center, geometric SYLORA wordmark, star in A and the full “YOUR AI. YOUR WORLD. YOUR LEGACY.” slogan. It must use `contain` and may never be cropped, redrawn or replaced by a text approximation.
- Navigation remains quiet, readable and stable. Desktop uses the left rail as the primary map; mobile uses exactly five persistent destinations — Home / LIVE / Sylora / Inbox / Profile — plus contextual secondary controls.
- Sylora is a living presence with portrait, voice state and contextual actions. Her presence may be visually prominent on a home/AI moment but must not permanently obstruct task content.
- Create is a recognisable contextual SYLORA object/state inside Home/Studio flows, not a sixth persistent mobile destination or a generic floating plus button.
- Motion should feel like light/air/material responding to the user: slow ambient flow, purposeful transitions, no constant noisy motion.
- Every major route has one quiet semantic motion scene: horizon/refraction for Home, signal/pulse for LIVE, media frames for Clips, preview/scan for Studio, voice orbit for Sylora, message packets for Inbox, personal orbit for Profile/Settings, and domain-specific restrained geometry elsewhere. All motion must respect the operating-system reduced-motion preference.
- TikTok, YouTube, OBS, TikFinity and RTMP(S) are visible interactive creator connections. Their labels must distinguish local relay, stream-key setup and real connected states; visual presence alone must never imply a live external connection.
- Interface language is a first-class Settings control for Ukrainian, English, Polish, German and Russian.

## Layout contract

Desktop preserves three functional depths where useful: stable navigation, primary working/content space, and contextual realtime/people/AI information. The exact columns can collapse when the task requires focus (LIVE, Studio, AI, editing).

Mobile is not a scaled desktop screenshot. It preserves the same visual DNA but reorders information around thumb reach, camera/video safe zones and a compact bottom navigation.

## Applies to every product area

Home/Feed, Clips/Video, LIVE, Creator Studio, AI Director, Sylora AI, Messages/Calls, Friends, Communities, Learning, Business, Wallet/Creator Economy, Gifts, Living World, LIVE Worlds, Story LIVE, Moments, Profile, Settings, Search/Explore, notifications and administration must inherit this design system.

## Acceptance rule

No redesigned tab is considered complete until desktop, tablet and phone screenshots are reviewed side-by-side with the canonical reference for palette, materials, spacing, navigation, typography, motion language, AI presence and visual density.

Functional completeness and visual completion are separate gates. Passing tests does not constitute design approval.
